          Settings and privacy

          Gemini AI

          Photo analysis and AI wording go through a small proxy that holds your GEMINI_API_KEY, so the key never reaches the browser. Without it, ROOMFIT uses its built-in parser and all fit checks still work.

          Your data

          Rooms and designs are saved only in this browser. Photos are never saved. Accounts and cloud sync are planned; see db/schema.sql.
